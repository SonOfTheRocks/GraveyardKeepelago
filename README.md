# Graveyard Keepelago

An [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) client for **Graveyard Keeper**.

Turn technology research and quest rewards into multiworld checks. Receive randomized
recipes, blueprints, gathering abilities and upgrades from across the multiworld,
while your discoveries help other players progress.

**Under active development.** Use matching client and APWorld versions.

## Getting started

Requires Graveyard Keeper on Windows, **BepInEx 5.x x64**, and any DLC enabled in your seed.

1. Get the [client](https://github.com/SonOfTheRocks/GraveyardKeepelago/releases) and matching [APWorld](https://github.com/SonOfTheRocks/GraveyardKeeper_APWorld).
2. Follow the [installation guide](docs/player-guide.md#installation).
3. Connect to your multiworld from the game's title screen, then start a new game. Existing normal saves cannot be converted into AP saves.
